# Account and Security

## Resetting your password

Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your account email. You will receive a reset link that expires after 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and make sure you used the address your account was created with.

## Two-factor authentication

We strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) in Settings > Security. Nimbus Drive supports authenticator apps and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not supported. When you enable 2FA you receive 10 one-time backup codes; store them somewhere safe.

## Lost access to two-factor authentication

If you lose your authenticator device, sign in with one of your backup codes. If you have no backup codes, contact support from your account email. For security, identity verification takes 3 to 5 business days and our team will never ask for your password.

## Encryption

All files are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Team plans can enable end-to-end encrypted folders, where encryption keys never leave your devices. Files in end-to-end encrypted folders cannot be previewed in the browser or recovered by support if you lose your keys.

## Data location

Data for accounts created in the EU is stored in Frankfurt, Germany. All other accounts are stored in Oregon, USA. Team plan admins can request a data location change by contacting support; migration takes up to 7 days.

## Active sessions

You can see every device signed in to your account under Settings > Security > Active sessions and sign out any of them remotely.
