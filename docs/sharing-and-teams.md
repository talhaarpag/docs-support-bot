# Sharing and Teams

## Sharing links

Right-click any file or folder and choose "Share link". Links can be view-only or allow editing. Pro and Team plans can add a password and an expiry date to a link. Free plan links never expire and cannot be password protected.

## Sharing limits

A single shared link can be downloaded up to 200 GB per day on the Pro plan and 1 TB per day on the Team plan. Free plan links are limited to 5 GB per day. When the limit is reached, the link is paused until the next day (UTC).

## Inviting team members

Team plan admins invite members from Admin Console > Members > Invite. Invitations expire after 7 days. Each new member is added to the next invoice at a prorated price.

## Roles

The Team plan has three roles. Owners manage billing and can delete the team. Admins manage members, shared folders and security settings. Members can use storage and share files according to the policies set by admins.

## Removing a member

When an admin removes a member, the member's files are transferred to an admin of your choice. The removed user keeps a Free plan account with no access to team folders.
