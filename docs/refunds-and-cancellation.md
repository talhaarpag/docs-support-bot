# Refunds and Cancellation

## Refund policy

New subscribers can request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment. After 14 days, monthly plans are not refundable. Annual plans cancelled after 14 days receive a prorated refund for the remaining full months, minus a 10% processing fee.

## How to request a refund

Open Settings > Billing and click "Request refund", or email billing@nimbusdrive.example with your account email and invoice number. Refunds are processed within 5 business days and returned to the original payment method. Bank transfer refunds can take up to 10 business days.

## Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Your paid features remain active until the end of the current billing period, after which the account moves to the Free plan. Cancelling does not delete your files.

## What happens to my data after cancellation

After moving to the Free plan, files above the 15 GB Free limit stay available in read-only mode for 90 days. After 90 days, files over the limit are permanently deleted, starting with the largest files. We send email reminders 30 days and 7 days before deletion.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account and all data, go to Settings > Account > Delete account. Deletion is permanent after a 30-day grace period, during which you can restore the account by signing in.
