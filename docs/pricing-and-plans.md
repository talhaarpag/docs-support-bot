# Pricing and Plans

## Available plans

Nimbus Drive offers three plans. The Free plan includes 15 GB of storage and supports one user. The Pro plan costs $9 per month and includes 2 TB of storage for one user. The Team plan costs $15 per user per month, includes 5 TB of pooled storage per user, and requires a minimum of 3 users.

## Annual billing

All paid plans can be billed annually. Annual billing gives a 20% discount compared to paying monthly. Annual plans are charged upfront for the full year.

## Changing your plan

You can upgrade at any time from Settings > Billing. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. If you downgrade and your stored data exceeds the new plan's limit, your account becomes read-only until you delete files or upgrade again.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Team plans billed annually can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice. We do not accept cryptocurrency.

## Taxes and invoices

Prices shown do not include VAT or sales tax, which is added at checkout based on your billing address. Invoices are available as PDF under Settings > Billing > Invoices. To add a company name or VAT number to invoices, edit your billing details before the next charge; past invoices cannot be changed.
